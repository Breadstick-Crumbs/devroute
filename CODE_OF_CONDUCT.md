# Code of Conduct

DevRoute aims to be a welcoming, professional project for everyone who uses or improves it.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Back technical claims with evidence, and be honest about uncertainty.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time with clear, focused issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's reporting tools or the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban participants, in proportion to the behavior. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from the project.
